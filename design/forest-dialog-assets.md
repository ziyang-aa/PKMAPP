# 原生森林弹窗素材

2026-11-18：原生弹窗采用奶油纸纹、森林绿标题、叶片、圆角描边输入框和纵向按钮。旧纸纹保留，新版页面与弹窗引用独立的新纸纹。

- 生图工具：内置 `image_gen.imagegen`，新图生成，非 CLI。
- 原图：`../artwork/source/backgrounds/forest_paper_clean.png`
- Android 素材：`../app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/forest_paper_clean.webp`
- 使用位置：`ForestUi.paper()`、`ForestDialogBuilder.Paper`。

最终提示词：

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: subtle repeatable raster paper texture for the backgrounds and dialogs of a quiet forest watercolor Android app. Primary request: a completely empty square sheet of warm pale cream watercolor paper, evenly lit, fine soft natural paper fibers and very subtle tiny irregular watercolor grain, exceptionally low contrast so small dark green text is easy to read. Palette ivory #fffaf1 and warm cream #f5f1e7, no brown aging stains or large blotches. Flat orthographic scan, texture fills the entire frame uniformly, seamless appearance at all four edges, no vignette, no shadows, no lighting gradients. No objects, no plants, no characters, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no border. This is a functional unobtrusive background texture, not a scene or UI mockup.
